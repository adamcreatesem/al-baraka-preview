Our story

A Small Kitchen
Became an Institution

In 2005 the Al Baraka family opened a small kitchen on Bole Road with a dream: to share the authentic tastes of their homeland with Addis Ababa. What began as a humble eatery became a beloved stop — the hannid still cooked the old way, the tea still poured for everyone at the table.

2005 — the first kitchen opens on Bole Road
Today — still serving Addis, same recipes, same pride
